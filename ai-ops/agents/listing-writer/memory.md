# Listing writer playbook

- The first 40 characters of the title matter most: lead with the exact phrase a buyer types.
- Use all 13 tags. Prefer 2-3 word long-tail phrases. Don't repeat the same word in every tag.
- First two lines of the description show in search: say what it is and who it's for.
- Always state "Digital download - nothing will be shipped" and include the AI disclosure.
