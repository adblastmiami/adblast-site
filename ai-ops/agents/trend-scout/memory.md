# Trend scout playbook

- Seasonal searches build 4-8 weeks before the holiday. Propose seasonal items at least 6 weeks early.
- Narrow niches convert better than generic ones ("realtor open house flyer" beats "flyer template").
- Editable templates and bundles are growing; plain single-page static PDFs are declining.
- Buyers avoid things that look AI-generated. Favor useful, specific products over decorative AI art.
- Our edge: we run a direct-mail business (AdBlast Miami). EDDM postcard and door-hanger templates have little competition.
