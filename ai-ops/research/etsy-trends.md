# Etsy trend research (as of Oct 12, 2026)

## What the market says
- **Best-selling digital categories:** printables, planners, Canva templates, SVG/PNG cut files and wall art. Wall art buyers often buy several prints at once.
- **Editable beats static.** Editable templates (Canva, Corjl) and fillable PDFs are growing, while plain static PDFs are declining.
- **Niche beats generic.** "Weekly planner printable" has huge competition. Narrow, specific products convert much better.
- **Buyers avoid products that look like AI.** A clean, professional, human-curated look sells at a premium. Our agents must curate and design rather than dump raw image-generator output.
- **Bundles and suites win.** This model makes money on volume and bundles, not high prices per item.
- **Timing.** Holiday searches build 4–8 weeks ahead, and Christmas searches dominate by October. **We are already inside the Q4 window, so speed matters.**
- **Trending 2026 aesthetics:** "Gothmas" (Halloween-meets-Christmas) and "Nutcrackercore" (reported ~1000% YoY search growth). Use them as styles for original designs, never copy.
- **Policy.** Etsy allows AI-assisted items, but they must be **disclosed** and labeled with how much the seller was involved. The "designed by seller" framing fits us. The owner should read Etsy's Creativity Standards page before launch.

## Open questions to check before launch
- How editable templates are delivered. Canva templates are shared as a link inside the PDF and need a free Canva account. Agents can make PDF/PNG/SVG directly, but turning a design into a Canva template may need a manual step by the owner or a Canva API check.
- The exact Etsy AI disclosure wording and attribute, from Etsy's official Creativity Standards page.
