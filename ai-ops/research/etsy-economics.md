# Etsy economics (US seller, digital downloads)

## Fees (2026, per third-party fee guides — verify on etsy.com before launch)
| Fee | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Listing | $0.20 | When a listing is created, then every 4 months it renews, and again each time a multi-quantity item sells |
| Transaction | 6.5% of the sale price | Every sale |
| Payment processing | 3% + $0.25 | Every order |
| Offsite Ads | 15% (12% for shops above $10k/yr), capped at $100/order | Only on sales that come from Etsy's offsite ads. These are **mandatory for shops above $10k/yr** and optional below that |
| Shop setup | About $15–29, one time (reported for new shops) | Once |
| Etsy Ads (onsite) | Your own daily budget | Optional, and **only with owner approval** |

## Profit per sale (no ads)
Fees per sale ≈ $0.45 + 9.5% of the price. This counts the $0.20 renewal on each sale.

| Price | Etsy fees | **You keep** | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| $3 | $0.74 | **$2.27** | 76% |
| $5 | $0.93 | **$4.08** | 82% |
| $8 | $1.21 | **$6.79** | 85% |
| $12 | $1.59 | **$10.41** | 87% |
| $19 | $2.26 | **$16.75** | 88% |
| $29 | $3.21 | **$25.80** | 89% |
| $39 | $4.16 | **$34.85** | 89% |

If a sale came through Offsite Ads, subtract another 15% of the price.

**Takeaway:** cheap items lose a large share of the price to the fixed fees. Bundles of $8 or more are where the profit is, so the $3 items exist to win first sales and reviews.

## Break-even on the $50/mo budget
- At an average sale of $8 → about **8 sales/month** to cover costs.
- At an average sale of $15 (with bundles mixed in) → about **4 sales/month**.

## What success looks like at scale (illustrative, not a forecast)
| Listings | Avg. sales/listing/month | Avg. net/sale | Monthly net |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 0.3 | $8 | $240 |
| 200 | 0.5 | $10 | $1,000 |

Most of the revenue usually comes from a small share of winning listings. The analyst agent's job is to find them and make variations of them.
